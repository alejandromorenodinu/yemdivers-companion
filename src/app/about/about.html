About

What is this?

This is an unofficial, fan-made companion app for Helldivers 2. It shows the state of the galactic war, the current Major Order, dispatches from Super Earth and an interactive galactic map.

Why did I make it?

I wanted a companion app built exactly the way I like it, and I wanted a hands-on project to gain real experience on Angular Framework.

This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Arrowhead Game Studios or Sony Interactive Entertainment in any way. Helldivers and all related names, characters and assets are the property of their respective owners. This is a non-commercial fan-made project!.

Where the data comes from

All game data is provided by the community-run Helldivers 2 API (helldivers-2/api). The information may be delayed, incomplete or inaccurate, and it should not be treated as official.
